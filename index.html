HourLedger Privacy Policy

App: HourLedger (com.itcarepoint.hourledger) · Publisher: IT Care Point · Last updated: November 13, 2026

HourLedger is an hours and timesheet tracker. It keeps your work hours on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access.

What the app stores

Your jobs, hourly rates, shifts, breaks, notes and settings, and the name you choose to print on PDF timesheets. This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. IT Care Point does not receive, see or sell it.

What is shared

The app does not send any data anywhere. It does not request internet access. A PDF, CSV or backup file leaves your phone only when you save or share it yourself, to the place or app you choose.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may copy the app's data to your own Google account, as it does for other apps. This is controlled by you in your phone's settings and is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the running clock while you are clocked in, and reminders you turn on.
  • Run at startup: to put the clock-in notification back after the phone restarts.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or any files other than those you choose.

Deleting your data

You can delete jobs and shifts in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Children

HourLedger is meant for working adults and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your data, for example if ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
